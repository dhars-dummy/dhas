## A better application process

Do not do:

* Apply to 200 jobs with the same resume
* Click only “Easy Apply”
* Wait for recruiter messages

Do this:

### Step 1: Create 3 resumes

**Resume A**

* Full Stack + Backend

**Resume B**

* AI/ML + GenAI

**Resume C**

* Software Engineer general

For example:

If applying to Backend roles:

* Move FastAPI, Django, APIs higher
* Move AI items lower

If applying to AI roles:

* Put AI projects first

---

### Step 2: Apply directly on company career pages

Target:

* 10–15 applications/day
* 70–100 targeted applications/week

Spreadsheet columns:

| Company | Role | Date | Referral asked? | Status |
| ------- | ---: | ---: | --------------: | -----: |

---

### Step 3: Use LinkedIn differently

Do not ask:

> "Can you refer me?"

Send:

> Hi, I saw you work at [company]. I’m a 2026 AI & Data Science student with experience in React, FastAPI and GenAI projects. I’m interested in Software Engineer roles. I applied for [role]. Could you share any advice on improving my chances?

After they reply:

> Thanks. If you feel my profile fits, I’d appreciate a referral.

This gets more responses.

---

### Step 4: Use GitHub as part of your resume

Your profile should contain:

* 3 pinned projects
* Proper README
* Screenshots
* Demo link
* Deployment link

Many startup founders check GitHub before resumes.

---

### Step 5: Daily preparation schedule

**2 hours**

* DSA (LeetCode)

**1 hour**

* System design basics

**1 hour**

* Build/improve project

**30 minutes**

* Applications + networking
